# Loft Conversion Accelerator

Hi lovable, Im revamping a website 'supremelofts.co.uk' Build a high-converting homepage for Supreme Lofts, a London loft conversion company with 20+ years' experience. The current site buries their best proof points and uses passive CTAs ("Contact Us", "Learn More") everywhere. Fix both. Every CTA in this build must be specific and action-oriented, tied to an outcome — never generic.

Design direction

Clean, modern, mobile-first, fast-loading

Trustworthy but not corporate-cold — this is a big-money home decision, so tone should reassure, not just impress

Deep navy or charcoal base + one warm accent colour (amber/terracotta) for CTAs and highlights

Generous whitespace, real photography over illustration/stock feel

Section-by-section spec

1. Hero

Full-width photo of their best completed loft conversion (high ceiling, good natural light)

Headline: specific to the service, not generic ("Loft Conversions Built Around Your Home" — not "Building & Construction")

One CTA button only: "Get My Free Quote"

2. Trust bar (place immediately under hero — this is the priority fix)

Horizontal strip, icon + short label for each:

20+ years' experience

10-year guarantee

£2m public liability insurance

Qualified & insured trades

One point of contact throughout

Planning & building-regs experience

Broad local coverage across London

3. Reviews

3–4 specific reviews pulled onto the homepage (not a subpage)

Include first name + job type per review (e.g. "Sarah, Dormer Loft Conversion — Walthamstow")

CTA under the block: "Read More Reviews"

4. Portfolio grid

Real before/after photo pairs, grouped by conversion type (dormer, hip-to-gable, mansard)

Each project card: CTA "See This Project" → expands or links to detail

5. One point of contact

Short section explaining Supreme Lofts manages every trade (carpentry, electrical, plumbing, plastering, tiling) under one dedicated contact — removes the anxiety of juggling multiple tradespeople

CTA: "Talk to My Project Manager"

6. Services

Condense to icon-led cards, one line each, not a text wall

Each card CTA: "Check If This Fits My Home"

7. FAQ (accordion, not buried at page bottom)

Use this copy as-is:

How much does a loft conversion cost? 

Every property is different. The cost depends on the type of conversion, size, structural requirements, specification and finishing choices. Contact us to discuss your property and requirements.

Do I need planning permission? 

Not necessarily. Some loft conversions may fall under permitted development, while others require planning permission. Requirements depend on the property and proposed works.

How long does a loft conversion take?

 The timescale depends on the complexity and specification of the project. We'll discuss the expected programme with you before work begins.

Do you manage all the trades? 

Yes. One of the advantages of working with Supreme Lofts is that we can provide the skilled trades required for the project, including carpentry, electrical, plumbing, plastering, tiling and more.

Is your work guaranteed? 

Yes. Every project is guaranteed for a minimum of 10 years.

8. Quote form (sticky or persistent, not buried behind clicks)

Fields: name, phone/email, property postcode, brief description

Include a photo/plan upload field so homeowners can get a faster, more accurate estimate without a call first

Submit button: "Get My Instant Estimate"

Technical

Fully responsive, mobile-first

Fast load — optimise images, avoid heavy carousels/animations that slow first paint
